Why We Built It

A practical portal for everyday mess ordering on campus.

This website helps students place food orders more smoothly while also making order handling easier for mess owners. It reduces confusion at pickup, keeps order flow organized, and supports a better experience even during busy mess hours.

Reliable Even in the Mess

The portal helps overcome the common problem of the internet not working properly inside the mess area during ordering or pickup.

Safer Payments

It also avoids the need to rely on mess Wi-Fi for payments, because using public or shared Wi-Fi for online payments is not secure.

Clearer for Everyone

Students get a smoother ordering experience, and owners get a clearer system to verify orders quickly using the generated order ID.
